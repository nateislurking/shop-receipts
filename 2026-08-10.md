**10:36 PM — MACHINE:**

> Good news first: the tell-it-something test passed cleanly, and the breadcrumb fix is **cheaper than the "copy file paths into each area" framing** — because there are no per-part breadcrumb files to copy. Here's what I found.
>
> [...]
>
> Breadcrumbs aren't baked into the machines' view files — they're **app chrome that `renderer.js` draws around whatever's open.** The demo pages actually go out of their way to *hide* their own crumbs (`demo.html:163` — `.demo-face [class*="crumb"]{display:none !important;}`). So the crumb you see on a preview is the app's, wrapped around the page.
>
> [...]
>
> The reframe that saves you the back-and-forth you're worried about: this is one `renderer.js` change per surface, not eleven file edits. Household-maintenance is just the machine to *watch* it on — nothing gets copied part-by-part, so there's no risk of a later conversion undoing it. Your test plan (prove on household-maintenance, then it's already applied to all) is exactly right and cheaper than it sounds.
